# EverySpend typographic landing

Preserve the original oversized EverySpend wordmark, quiet navigation, fine divider, editorial intro and full-width bottom pouch. Graphite colours, neutral light mode, serif accent and one Google Play download control. No decorative icons, feature cards or framed mascot.

The header, intro and artwork occupy separate rows in normal document flow. The artwork is bottom-aligned when space permits; short screens scroll rather than clipping content. Its intrinsic 3:1 ratio is preserved. Phones use a deliberate wider composition with the entire central face visible, while the outer shoulders extend beyond the page edges.

References: Haus Otto (https://www.hausotto.com/) for bold typographic restraint; web.dev (https://web.dev/articles/aspect-ratio) for preserving image proportions rather than forcing a fixed-height cover crop.

The pouch now has a separate eye-free animation base and fixed white eye shapes with clipped dark pupils; only the pupils move, by at most seven artwork pixels. A restrained CSS blink runs every 7.2 seconds. Pointer gaze uses the installed Motion springs; idle glances look up toward the wordmark, sideways, and forward at irregular intervals; phones keep idle glances and blinking without pointer tracking. Reduced-motion preferences disable both. The responsive stage is larger while preserving the original full-width composition.

The current hero pairs sans-serif Every with italic serif Spend in a single oversized wordmark. The intro is an offset two-column composition with the download below the supporting copy; the divider is removed. Phones stack the intro while preserving the mixed-type brand.
