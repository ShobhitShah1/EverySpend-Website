# EverySpend single-screen landing

A large typographic EverySpend wordmark leads the page, with a quiet divider and an editorial intro/download row below. The small top header carries the brand line and support links. Full-width pouch remains at the bottom, no coin animation. Graphite colours, neutral light mode, saved theme and reduced-motion support.

Phones stack the intro and download beneath the wordmark. Pouch height is constrained by screen width and height to preserve its face. All original artwork is retained.

Intro starts near the top rather than anchoring to the artwork. Download includes a Google Play mark. Phones use a larger wordmark and a consistent-width download control with aligned icon and arrow.
