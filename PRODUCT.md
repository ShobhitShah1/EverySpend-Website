# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to track everyday spending, people building budgeting and savings habits, and people who want help organizing bank transaction messages.

## Product Purpose

EverySpend is an Android app for keeping everyday expenses and money plans together. Visitors should understand how it can help and have a clear path to install it.

## Positioning

Users can track expenses manually; SMS import is optional and transaction messages are processed on-device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Android app.
- Manual expense tracking is available.
- SMS import is optional and is processed on-device.
- Do not imply that SMS access is required.
- Product details beyond these confirmed capabilities should be verified before they are presented as fact.

## Evidence on Hand

- The site includes illustrative app previews in `src/components/EverySpendLanding.tsx`.
- Pouch artwork used by the site is in `public/images/`.
- Illustrative preview data is not a product-performance claim or customer evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make everyday money tracking approachable.
- Keep manual tracking available.
- Make SMS import a choice, not a prerequisite.
- Explain confirmed capabilities plainly; do not invent claims.
